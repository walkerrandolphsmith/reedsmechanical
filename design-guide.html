Logo Iconography
The Arch Mark is built from two ideas held in tension: a rising half-sun of heat rays above, and a cool water-air drop below. Together they communicate the full scope of HVAC work instantly — no industry knowledge required. The mark should never be altered, redrawn, or reconstructed from parts.

Color System
The palette is anchored in warmth. Rust-orange is the primary brand color — it communicates heat, craft, and confidence. Cool blue plays counterpart, representing the cooling side of HVAC. All neutral backgrounds carry a subtle warm undertone; never use a pure cold gray or pure black.

Color — Do
  • Use Rust on Ink backgrounds as the primary action/accent color
  • Pair Blue only as a counterpoint to Rust — never use blue alone as the hero
  • Use Warm White (#F0ECE6) for all body and headline text on dark backgrounds
  • Use Paper or Cream for all light background surfaces
  • Keep Amber strictly to the icon's sun core — never in type or UI
Color — Don't
  • Don't use pure black (#000000) or pure white (#FFFFFF) anywhere
  • Don't use Blue as a hero or primary brand color — it is always secondary
  • Don't use Rust on a light background without switching to Rust Dark (#B53A08)
  • Don't introduce any new colors outside this palette
  • Don't use Warm Gray for headlines — body and labels only
03
Font Family & Rules
Two typeface families — both from the Barlow superfamily — create a cohesive but expressive system. Barlow Condensed handles all display and brand moments. Inter handles everything functional. The italic sub-label is a deliberate personality injection; it's the only place italics appear in the system.

Typography — Do
  • Set REED in Barlow Condensed 900 — the wordmark never uses any lighter weight
  • Set MECHANICAL with generous tracking (0.18em–0.22em) in Barlow Condensed 700
  • Reserve Barlow Italic strictly for the sub-label and tagline contexts
  • Use Inter 300 for metadata, Inter 400 for body, Inter 500 for names/labels
  • Always uppercase Barlow Condensed; always sentence-case Barlow Italic
Typography — Don't
  • Don't use Barlow Condensed at weights below 700 in any brand context
  • Don't use italic type anywhere except the designated sub-label position
  • Don't use Inter Bold (700+) — it competes with Barlow's visual weight
  • Don't introduce a third typeface for any brand materials
  • Don't set body copy in Barlow Condensed — that family is display-only
04
Business Cards
Two card variants — Dark (primary) and Light (secondary). Both are 3.5 × 2 inches. The dark card is the default for most use; the light card works on surfaces where a dark card wouldn't stand out. Both fronts carry the full logo. Both backs offer a clean service listing with optional QR zone.
